# 20 — Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery

Run a business-impact analysis for connectivity, identity, communications,
payments, records, and service delivery. Set recovery time and recovery point
objectives, document manual workarounds, verify backups, and exercise a
provider outage without interrupting real services.

**Evidence:** dependency map, prioritized services, recovery runbook, contact
tree, tabletop decision log, and after-action improvements.
