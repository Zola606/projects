# 19 — Third-Party Security Risk Review

Build a lightweight review covering data location, access, MFA, incident
notification, subcontractors, backups, recovery objectives, vulnerability
management, and evidence. Score inherent and residual risk, record accepted
gaps, and schedule reassessment based on criticality.

**Evidence:** synthetic questionnaire, scoring rubric, contract-control
checklist, remediation tickets, and executive risk acceptance.
