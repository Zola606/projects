# 07 — Voice and data VLAN quality

**Real problem:** a clinic or NGO with limited connectivity needs reliable
voice while users share the link. Put phones and PCs in separate VLANs,
provide the correct DHCP voice options supported by the lab image, and model
priority treatment for voice traffic.

**Validate:** phone registration in the simulation, separate addressing,
inter-VLAN policy, and a congestion test with recorded observations.
